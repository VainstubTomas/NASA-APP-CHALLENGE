# ADR-000: Short title of the decision

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Replaced by ADR-N
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decided by**: names

## Context
What problem or question there is, and what constraints we have.

## Decision
What we decided, in one or two clear sentences.

## Discarded alternatives
- Alternative A: why not.
- Alternative B: why not.

## Consequences
What changes for the team, what we gain and what cost we accept.
